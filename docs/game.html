The game

Radical Entertainment's open-world game picks up in the last scene of the 1983 film, but this time Tony Montana survives the attack on his mansion. Stripped of everything, he works his way back up through Miami's drug trade: territory, fronts, money to launder, gangs to wipe out, and Sosa at the end of it.

It came out in 2006 for the PlayStation 2, Xbox and PC, published by Vivendi Games under the Sierra name, and on the Wii in 2007. An Xbox 360 version was started and dropped. In October 2025 EC Digital Entertainment brought the PC version back, on Steam and the Epic Games Store.

Play it today

The 2006 PC release doesn't run well on today's computers out of the box. These fix that, and the emulators run the console versions from your own discs.

SilentPatch for Scarface

by Silent (CookiePLMonster) · PC

The fix to start with. It stops the graphics corruption the game shows on modern machines, makes it run noticeably faster, and lists every resolution your screen supports. It also brings the ASI loader that mods such as ScarfaceHook need.

SourceHow he fixed it

Scarface Fusion Fix

by ThirteenAG · PC

Fixes the mouse look and the controller support, and lets you drive and shoot with the triggers like a modern game. Part of his Widescreen Fixes Pack; look for the Scarface release.

PCGamingWiki

community wiki · PC

The PC version's known problems and their fixes, kept up to date by the community. The first place to look when something goes wrong.

xemu

open-source original Xbox emulator · Windows, macOS, Linux

Runs the Xbox version, the one this project rebuilds first. It needs files from your own console and a copy of your own disc; its site explains how to make them.

PCSX2

PlayStation 2 emulator · the game's page on its wiki

The PS2 version runs on PCSX2. The game's page on the PCSX2 wiki lists the settings people use and the known problems, such as shadows that go missing.

The 2025 re-release

EC Digital Entertainment · Steam, Epic Games Store

The PC game is on sale again. Silent noted that the early Epic release seems to ship with SilentPatch already, so check what yours includes before adding fixes on top.

Mods and tools

What the modding scene has built for the game. Get each one from its author's own page.

Scarface Remastered Project

by Neoxaero, with sulzbals, Talon and DomenicoPe · PC

The big one: keeps the PC game playable and looking its best. The community this project grew up beside.

ScarfaceHook

by ermaccer · PC

An in-game menu with a long list of options for changing how the game plays: the camera, the time of day, spawning cars and people, a teleporter. Needs SilentPatch's loader.

Source

Gangsta Team

reverse engineering and modding · PC 1.00.2

GangstaPatch (an unofficial patch), GangstaMenu (a mod menu) and RCFRedirect, which lets the game load files from a folder instead of its .rcf archives. Also a list of the game's script commands and an archive explorer that runs in the browser.

Script commandsRCF explorer

ScarfaceEX

A mod built on the game's own script commands, credited to many people of the Scarface modding scene.
